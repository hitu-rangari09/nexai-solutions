# Pre-Publish Checklist

Run through this **yourself** before you upload. The upload-preparation agent fills in
the draft material; you are the one who publishes, and you are the one responsible for
what goes out.

> **Policies change.** YouTube's Community Guidelines, monetization rules, copyright
> and fair-use handling, and the rules for disclosing AI-generated / altered /
> synthetic content are updated regularly and differ by country. Nothing in this repo
> is legal advice and nothing here is guaranteed to be current. **Check the official
> YouTube / Google policy pages and your local law before you publish.**

---

## 1. Accuracy
- [ ] Every factual claim in the video traces to a `[F#]` / `[N#]` entry in `research.json`
- [ ] Every source in that file still resolves and still says what we said it says
- [ ] Anything unverified is either labeled as unverified in the video or cut
- [ ] No invented numbers, dates, quotes, or statistics
- [ ] The title and thumbnail promise something the video actually delivers

## 2. Rights and copyright
- [ ] Music: license verified, and I can produce proof of it
- [ ] Footage / stock / images: license verified, attribution added where required
- [ ] Fonts: license permits this use
- [ ] No third-party logos, trademarks, or product designs used without permission
- [ ] No real person's likeness or voice used without their documented permission
- [ ] AI-generated visuals contain no copyrighted characters or brand marks
- [ ] Any clip used under a fair-use argument: I understand fair use is decided case by
      case, is not a guarantee, and does not stop a claim or strike from happening

## 3. AI disclosure
- [ ] Checked YouTube's **current** rules on disclosing altered or synthetic content
- [ ] Answered the "altered or synthetic content" question in the upload flow honestly
- [ ] AI voice / AI visuals disclosed in the description if applicable
- [ ] No AI-generated depiction of a real, identifiable person doing something they didn't do

## 4. Content safety
- [ ] "Made for Kids" declaration answered honestly — this is a legal declaration
- [ ] No medical / legal / financial advice presented as fact; professional-advice pointer present
- [ ] No claims about the viewer's results, income, or outcomes
- [ ] Nothing that could read as harassment, misinformation, or a dangerous act
- [ ] Age-restriction considered if the topic warrants it

## 5. Monetization (if applicable)
- [ ] Checked **current** YouTube Partner Program eligibility and advertiser-friendly
      guidelines — these change, and eligibility is not something this system can confirm
- [ ] Self-certified ad suitability honestly
- [ ] Any sponsorship or affiliate link disclosed in-video and in the description, per
      YouTube's paid-promotion rules and your local advertising law

## 6. Technical
- [ ] Audio normalized, no clipping, no dead air at the head
- [ ] Captions checked for mistranscriptions
- [ ] Thumbnail legible at 210×118 and in dark mode
- [ ] Chapters start at `0:00` and are in ascending order, each ≥ 10 seconds
- [ ] Description links verified by hand
- [ ] End screen / cards don't cover important content

## 7. Final
- [ ] I have read the description and the pinned comment draft in full
- [ ] I am publishing this myself — no automation is doing it for me
- [ ] After uploading, I set `published: true` and `video_url` in `upload.json` myself

**No part of this checklist can be completed by an agent on your behalf.**
