### Voice rules
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Concrete over abstract. Name the thing.
- No hype words: *insane, crazy, secret, hack, guaranteed, life-changing, blow up*.
- No promises about the viewer's results.
- Say "I don't know" / "this is contested" when the research says so.

## 6. Hard limits for this channel

- No copyrighted music, footage, images, clips, logos, or voice likenesses without a
  license or written permission the owner can produce on request.
- No claims of results, income, or outcomes for the viewer.
- No medical / legal / financial advice as fact — general information only, with a
  "talk to a professional" pointer.
- No fabricated personal anecdotes presented as real.
- AI voice and AI visuals are disclosed. Assume disclosure is required unless the
  owner has personally verified current YouTube policy says otherwise.
- Anything the research could not verify is labeled as unverified in the script, or cut.
