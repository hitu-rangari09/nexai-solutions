Our Story

NexAI Solutions was born from a simple observation: AI technology is transforming businesses globally, but most Indian SMEs are left behind due to high costs and complexity.

We started in Nagpur with a vision to change that. By combining open-source AI tools, no-code platforms, and deep technical expertise, we've made it possible for businesses of all sizes to leverage AI - without breaking the bank.

Today, we've helped 50+ businesses automate their operations, engage customers better, and scale faster. And we're just getting started.
